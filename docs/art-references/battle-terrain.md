# Battle terrain (isometric pixel ground)

Studied for `src/client/render/ground.ts` (procedural ground) and the props in `fx.ts`. Reference only; no pixels copied.

| # | Source | Licence | Taken from it |
|---|--------|---------|---------------|
| 1 | [Isometric pixel terrain](https://opengameart.org/content/isometric-pixel-terrain) (`isometric_0.png`) | CC0 | Grass reads through a sprinkle of single lighter pixels, not smooth noise; tufts are small dark "v" clusters; trees have a dark side and a dark cast shadow on the grass; a trodden path is a checker-dithered band with soft ragged edges. |
| 2 | [Isometric ground tiles](https://opengameart.org/content/isometric-ground-tiles) (rubberduck, `prev_main_0.png`) | CC0 | Ground is zones of material (dirt, lush grass, dry yellow grass, gravel road) with irregular borders; the road carries scattered pale pebbles; dry grass is its own ochre-yellow zone, not a tint. |
| 3 | [Isometric Ground Set](https://opengameart.org/content/isometric-ground-set) (hernandack, `cover-iso-ground-pack-1.png`) | CC0 | Dry earth: warm ochre base with fine dark/light speckle; dead shrubs as 3-4 dark strokes; 3-4 colours per material. |
| 4 | [Stylized grassland tileset](https://opengameart.org/content/stylized-grassland-tileset) (rubberduck, `grassland_tileset_prev_0.png`) | CC0 | Tufts gather in clumps with bare stretches between; pebbles scattered near rock; rocks lit from the upper left with dark undersides. |

Applied: a warmer, drier Levant palette (ochre-dominant, olive grass in zones), tufts placed in clumps, pale pebbles along the road, props lit from the upper left with a dark side and a one-pixel dark outline.
