# Undead (The Hollow Host): references

Studied for the Undead faction (docs/factions.md). Reference only; no image files committed.

| # | Image | Source / licence | Taken from it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hans Holbein the Younger, *The Knight*, from *The Dance of Death* (Met 336310) | https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/336310 — image https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/dp/web-large/DP-23045-001.jpg — Public domain (Met Open Access) | Death fights **in scraps of armour**: a mail shirt hanging off a ribcage, a helmet over a skull, bare shin bones. The skeleton is lanky and **knock-kneed**, the head pushed forward. The undead keep the shapes of the living soldiers (helmet, shield, spear) with gaps where flesh should be; that is how we keep them grim rather than cartoonish. |
| 2 | Dirck Volckertsz Coornhert, *The Triumph of Death* (Met 408284) | https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/408284 — image https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/dp/web-large/DP823116.jpg — Public domain | A flying skeleton with a scythe over a field of the dead. Long thin weapon **diagonals** dominate the silhouette; the ribcage is a block of horizontal light lines; the hair/rags stream behind. For the Lich-Marshal: tall, scythe or staff, rags trailing. The corpses below are just limbs and fabric, no gore: our PG-13 line. |
| 3 | Wenceslaus Hollar, *The Knight*, after Holbein (Met 361663) | https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/361663 — image https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/dp/web-large/DP822859.jpg — Public domain | Second take on the same scene; confirms the read: skull + dark eye sockets + a few rib lines is enough. Death holds a spear like an ordinary soldier, so our skeletons hold weapons with the same poses as the Templars (shared animation timing). |
| 4 | Utagawa Kuniyoshi, *Ghosts of the Taira at Daimotsu Bay* (Met 55743) | https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/55743 — image https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/as/web-large/DP146823.jpg — Public domain | Spectral warriors as **dark, translucent shapes in the sky** with pale accents: the look for wraiths (Wraith Bows) and the death-glow: dark body, a few pale/green pixels, edges that dissolve. |
| 5 | antumdeluge (rework of artisticdude), *Skeletons rework* (OpenGameArt) | https://opengameart.org/content/skeletons-rework — image https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/preview_676.png — **CC0** | Skeletons at **~20–24 px**: a big 6–7 px skull (1/3 of the height), black eye sockets 1 px each, ribs as alternating white/black rows, pelvis as one bright pixel row, arms and legs 1 px wide with a dark outline. One variant has **red eye pixels**, which reads instantly; we use the faction green instead. A round wooden shield in front hides most of the body, so shields carry the team hue. |
| 6 | patvanmackelberg, *Skeleton and friends (8 directional)*, "Gate Keeper" (OpenGameArt) | https://opengameart.org/content/skeleton-and-friends-8-directional — image https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Gate%20Keeper%20Idle.png — **CC0** | The caster: a skull over a **black ragged robe that widens to the ground**, a tall gold-key staff and a **green glowing book**. Exactly the Lich-Marshal read: dark trapezoid robe, bone head, one green light source. Also confirms green as the necromantic accent against black. |
